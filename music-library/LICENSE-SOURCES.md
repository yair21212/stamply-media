# Music library — license sources

All tracks come from **Mixkit** (https://mixkit.co/free-stock-music/) and are covered by the **Mixkit Stock Music Free License** (https://mixkit.co/license/#musicFree).

## How the license was verified (24.09.2026)

- Mixkit has no separate page per music track: `/free-stock-music/<slug>-<id>/` redirects to a general "discover" page. The track appears only as a card on listing pages.
- Each listing page where a track was found states: *"All audio tracks are completely free and ready to be used in your next project under the Mixkit License"*, linking to `/license/#musicFree`. Every track's card has a "Download Free Music" button. The pages are listed per track below (`licenseVerifiedOn` in music-library.json).
- The license page (https://mixkit.co/license/) lists only **one** license for music: "Stock Music Free License". The Restricted license exists only for video (`videoRestricted`). The music Restricted modal (`/license/modal/musicRestricted/`) is empty.
- Tracks with no license evidence were not downloaded or included. All 456 candidates came from pages containing this statement.

## License terms (from https://mixkit.co/license/modal/musicFree/, 24.09.2026)

- **Allowed:** commercial and non-commercial use, online marketing ads, social media video posts, YouTube, podcasts, educational. No credit required.
- **Not allowed:** TV & radio broadcasts, CDs/DVDs, video games, remixing into a music-only track, claiming as your own, or registering on a rights-management service (Content ID).
- If a claim is received: forward it to team@mixkit.co.
